# Management workflow media

## SAP CAP future workflow concept

`iicp-management-sap-cap-future-workflow.mp4` illustrates a possible future
operator workflow for connecting an SAP CAP application to an IICP-managed
intelligence environment. It follows policy definition, validation, activation,
effective-policy inspection, provider selection and receipt reporting.

The recording is a design concept, not a demonstration of a released SAP
connector, production deployment or stable command syntax. `IIOS` is a
provisional user-experience name; the public architectural term remains **IICP
Management Environment**. SAP is used as an integration example and the video
does not imply endorsement, certification or affiliation.

Media facts:

- duration: 2 minutes 17 seconds;
- video: H.264, 1344 × 932, 30 frames per second;
- audio: AAC stereo, 48 kHz;
- SHA-256: `69fb99ce5a15317ebfee73d7ee940de57e1202293f8ba74b863112483b72fb50`.

The project maintainer confirmed that the visual and audio material may be
distributed publicly with this repository.
